About Lomo

Songs From The Heart

My name is Jim Lomonaco, but everyone calls me Lomo. I am a singer and songwriter who plays acoustic guitar and loves original music.

I got my start as a bassist on a variety of recordings and in bands, most notable are Yolk and Honker. I am currently the bassist in Caviar & Grits.

Now, I am finding my voice as a writer, singer and producer; trying to push my creative limits. As I am on my musical journey, I hope our souls make a connection. I have songs about ideas like

  • Travel and Exploration
  • Self Improvement
  • Relationships
  • Social Commentary

Solo Songwriting

I’m a singer / songwriter now

For as long as I have been playing, I have preferred to make original music. I have attached myself to some great writers in this pursuit as a bassist. Now is the time to tell my story and connect with my own audience. My debut album "Liquid Symphony" is available on all streaming platforms.

Yolk

Yolk is the group I was in during the 90’s

Yolk was a popular act in the 90’s, not just in our hometown but throughout the Northeastern US. Originally the brain-child of guitarist Phil Clark, Yolk went through several band names and lineups before calling it quits around 2000. Our music had many influences and is unique and original. We released three independent albums and toured the East Coast extensively.

Listen

Caviar & Grits

Caviar & Grits is my current R&B / Soul project

Lead by singer and writer Michaela Clark, Caviar & Grits is a fun band to be in. Our lineup includes some great, talented players and Michaela’s voice is amazing. We recently released our EP "The C&G Spot" and expect to gain more attention and opportunities to play for you soon!

Listen

Honker

Honker is my defunct funk band

After being out of music for several years, Honker is the group that got me back in. It started as some friends getting together to jam and turned into something way more special. Honker showed me I could write some decent material and every member plays at a high skill level. We wrote our own songs and then would extend them into sometimes ten or twenty minute sonic experiments when performing live.
